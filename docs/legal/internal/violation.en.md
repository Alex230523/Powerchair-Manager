# Personal data breach procedure

> Internal draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

1. **Find and contain**: cut the access involved, change the secrets, keep the traces (logs).
2. **Assess**: which data, which clubs, how many people, whether health data or data about minors are involved, the risk for the people.
3. **Tell every club concerned** (controller) without undue delay, at the latest 48 hours after becoming aware, with the template below. The club notifies the Data Protection Authority within 72 hours when needed, and the people when the risk is high.
4. **Fix and document**: measures taken, register of breaches.

## Template of the notice to the club
Subject: Personal data breach concerning your club

Hello,

On (date, time), we found (nature of the incident). Data concerned: (categories). People concerned: (approximate number). Likely consequences: (description). Measures taken: (description). Measures we suggest to you: (description). Contact: (name, email, phone).

We remain available to help you notify the Data Protection Authority and the people.
