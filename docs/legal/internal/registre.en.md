# Record of processing activities (as processor)

> Internal draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

GDPR article 30.2. One record per processor, kept for each client club (controller).

| Item | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Processor | The operator of Powerchair Football Manager (contact details and data protection officer, if appointed, to complete) |
| Controllers | Each client club, and the federation for its own data (list kept in the application: clubs and date the agreement was accepted) |
| Categories of processing | Members and teams; attendance; matches and statistics; documents; fees and accounting; equipment and loans; trips; communication and notifications; health data needed for the players' safety |
| Categories of people | Players (including minors), parents and legal representatives, coaches, volunteers, referees, contacts and partners |
| Special data | Health (classification, instructions, certificates); data about minors |
| Transfers outside the EU | None planned; hosting in the European Union (see the list of sub-processors) |
| Security measures | Rights by role, encryption of files and queued messages, log of access to health data, backups, retention periods with a planned purge |
| Periods | As each club sets them, within the application's bounds; accounting as the law requires |
