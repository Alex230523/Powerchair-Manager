# Data protection impact assessment (DPIA): health data and data about minors

> Internal draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

## Description
The service processes, for the clubs, health data of players with disabilities (classification, instructions, certificates) and data about minors. These categories call for an impact assessment.

## Necessity and proportionality
- Only the information needed for safety and for playing is asked for; the identity card is no longer collected.
- Health data is kept for the « sensitive documents » right and the roles that need it; a minor is followed by the legal representatives.

## Risks and measures
| Risk | Measures |
| --- | --- |
| Unauthorised access to health data | Rights by role, a log of each access, the « Security and data » page for the presidency |
| Loss or leak of files | Encrypted files, backups, hosting in the EU |
| Keeping data too long | Retention periods within bounds, a planned and logged purge |
| Rights hard to exercise | Requests in the application, sent to the club with a 30 day deadline; full export |
| End of the relationship | Closure with a 30 day delay, deletion and certificate |

## Conclusion
Residual risk to assess with the lawyer and, if needed, with the Data Protection Authority.
