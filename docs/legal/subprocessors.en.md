## Hosting region

Data and files are hosted in the European Union.

| Service | Role | Region |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application hosting | Servers running the service | UE |
| PostgreSQL database | Storage of data and files, encrypted | UE |
| Email sending (SMTP) | Delivery of email notifications | UE |
| Browser notifications (Web Push) | The user's browser notification service, with no readable content | Depends on the browser |
