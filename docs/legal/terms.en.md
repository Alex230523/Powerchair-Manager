# Terms of use of the service

> Draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

Version : 2026-11-02

## Who provides what

Powerchair Football Manager is an online management service for powerchair football clubs and federations. The operator provides the application, its hosting and its support.

Each club remains responsible for the data it records and for the way its members use it.

## Access

The club opens access to its members by invitation. Everyone keeps a personal account and only sees what their roles allow.

The club makes sure the roles it gives match real needs.

## Availability

The operator does its best to keep the service available and backed up, without any guarantee of permanent availability. Planned interruptions are announced in advance when possible.

## Responsibilities

The operator is liable for faults in running the service. It is not liable for the contents entered by the clubs nor for decisions taken from them.

The club is responsible for following the rules that apply to its activities (insurance, sports regulations, protection of minors).

## End of the contract

The club may stop at any time from Settings › Club › Security and data: a full export is offered, then its data is deleted after a 30 day cooling-off period, except the accounting the law requires to keep.

## Applicable law

These terms fall under Belgian law. In case of disagreement, the parties first look for an amicable solution.
