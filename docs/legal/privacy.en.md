# Privacy policy

> Draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

Version : 2026-11-02

- Your club decides which data is recorded and why; the operator processes it on the club's behalf.
- Health data and data about minors are kept for a few roles only, and every look at them is logged.
- Your data stays in the European Union and is never sold.
- You can ask for access, correction or erasure of your data from Settings › My data.

## Who is responsible

The club you belong to is the controller of your data. The operator of the service acts as processor, on its instructions, under a data processing agreement.

## Which data

Identity and contact details, roles and teams, attendance, sports results, documents, fees, and for players the information needed for their safety (classification, chair, health instructions) when the club asks for it.

For a minor, the legal representatives are linked to the account.

## Why

To run the club: trainings, matches, trips, equipment, communication, fees and administrative and sports obligations.

## How long

The club sets retention periods within bounds; after them, data is deleted or anonymised automatically. Accounting is kept as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection: ask from Settings › My data; the request goes to your club, which answers within the legal deadline. You may also contact the Data Protection Authority.

## Security

Protected sign-in, rights by role, encrypted files, a log of the access to health data, regular backups.
