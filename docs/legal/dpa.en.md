# Data processing agreement (GDPR article 28)

> Draft to be reviewed by a lawyer. This text is not legal advice.

Version : 2026-11-02

## Subject and duration

The club (controller) entrusts the operator (processor) with processing the data needed for the Powerchair Football Manager service, for as long as it uses the service.

## Categories of data and people

Members, players, parents, coaches, volunteers, referees and contacts of the club. Identity, contact, sports, administrative and financial data; players' health data (classification, instructions) and data about minors, special categories processed with reinforced measures.

## Instructions

The operator processes the data only on the club's documented instructions, that is through the features of the service and the club's settings. It tells the club when an instruction seems to break the rules.

## Confidentiality and security

The people allowed to process the data are bound to confidentiality. Measures: access by role, encryption of files and of queued messages, a log of access to health data, backups, hosting in the European Union.

## Sub-processors

The public list is kept up to date on the Sub-processors page. The club is told before any addition and may object; the operator imposes the same obligations on each of them.

## Assistance

The operator helps the club answer people's requests (requests tool, export, rectification, erasure), carry out an impact assessment and notify a breach.

## Personal data breach

The operator tells the club without undue delay, and at the latest within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, with the information available.

## End of the contract

At the end, the club takes back its data (full export); it is then deleted, except a legal duty to keep (accounting). A deletion certificate is handed over.

## Audit

The operator makes available the information needed to show these obligations are met.
